Your mouth tells the truth about your health long before the rest of your body does.

Most of the serious cases we treat began as something small and ignorable — a little sensitivity, a bit of bleeding while brushing, a tooth that only hurt sometimes. Dental problems stay quiet until they are expensive. Six months of good habits and one honest checkup is almost always cheaper, faster and far less painful than what comes after waiting.

Twice a day, two minutes

Brushing properly for two full minutes, morning and night, removes the plaque film that hardens into tartar within 48 hours. Most people brush for under 45 seconds.

Gums don't bleed for no reason

Bleeding while brushing isn't normal, and isn't caused by brushing too hard. It's usually the earliest visible sign of gum disease — and the easiest stage to reverse completely.

A checkup every six months

Decay and gum disease are painless in their early stages. A six-month checkup catches them while they still need a filling and a cleaning, not a root canal or an extraction.

Treatment Costs

What dental treatment
actually costs

Prices vary more than most people expect. Here's what genuinely drives the number — and why an honest figure needs an examination first.

Root canal treatment

The main variable is which tooth. A front tooth has a single canal; a molar can have three or four, and takes considerably longer. Cost also depends on whether there is existing infection to clear, and whether the tooth needs a crown afterwards to stop it fracturing — for back teeth, it usually does.

Dental implants

Driven by bone condition more than anything. If the bone has receded after a tooth has been missing a while, a graft is needed first and that adds both cost and months. The implant system used and the number of teeth being replaced account for the rest.

Braces & aligners

Depends on how far the teeth have to move and what you wear while they do. Metal braces, ceramic braces and clear aligners sit at different price points, and treatment typically runs twelve to twenty-four months — longer cases cost more because they need more visits.

You get the figure before anything starts. After the examination — and digital X-rays where they're needed — we tell you what the treatment will cost and why. Nothing begins until you've heard the number and agreed to it. If a cheaper option is reasonable for your case, we'll say so.
