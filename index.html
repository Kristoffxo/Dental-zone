The Doctor

Dr. Mukesh Basantani, BDS

Dental Surgeon & Implantologist. A graduate of Manipal Academy of Higher Education (2003), he went on to complete advanced training in orthodontics, implants and surgical extractions at national and international institutes.

He spent 12 years practising at a leading dental hospital in Uttar Pradesh, sharpening his hands on cases most young dentists never see, before founding Dental Zone on 23rd November — a super-speciality dental hospital built on one belief: that great dentistry should still feel personal.

What sets his practice apart isn't just the case count — it's the approach. Patients consistently mention that he takes the time to explain an X-ray before touching a drill, that he'll talk a nervous first-timer through their fear rather than rushing past it, and that his diagnoses are honest even when the easier thing would be to oversell a treatment. Two decades in, he still treats every new patient like the first.

Over the years, his focus has widened from general dentistry into three areas he's especially known for across Allahabad: complex root canal therapy, dental implants for patients who've lost teeth to age or injury, and full-mouth orthodontic correction — often for adult patients who assumed braces were "too late" for them.

  • Over 1,000 fixed orthodontic treatments completed, many for adult patients
  • 50,000+ root canals, implants and extractions performed across 23 years
  • 19 years working specifically as a specialist implantologist
  • Verified medical registration, 97% patient satisfaction across 450+ tracked patients
  • Advanced certifications in orthodontics, implantology and surgical extractions, both national and international
  • Founder and lead surgeon at Dental Zone since its founding on 23rd November
🏅 Felicitated with the Pride of Allahabad Award by the Honourable Governor of Uttar Pradesh, Shri Keshari Nath Tripathi — recognition for his contribution to dentistry in the region.

Common Questions

Before you book

Is root canal treatment painful?

No — with modern anesthesia and equipment, most patients describe it as no worse than a filling.

Do you treat children?

Yes, Dental Zone sees pediatric patients with a gentler, more patient approach suited to kids.

How much do implants cost?

Cost depends on tooth position and implant type — book a consultation for an exact quote.

Do you handle dental emergencies?

Yes — severe pain, swelling, or a broken tooth are treated as priority same-day cases.

How often should I get a checkup?

Every 6 months is recommended for cleaning and early problem detection.
